Privacy Policy for Shishumangal Clinic

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Shishumangal Clinic ("we", "our", or "the clinic") operates the Shishumangal Clinic Android application.

Information We Collect

The app does not require patients or general visitors to create an account or provide personal information to view clinic information.

The app may use Google Sign-In for authorized clinic administration. The administrator's Google account information is used only to authenticate access to the administrative features.

How We Use Information

Information used for administrator authentication is used to securely provide access to the clinic's administrative features.

Data Storage

Clinic information such as consultation fees, clinic notices, and chamber timings is stored using Google Firebase Firestore so that authorized clinic administrators can update the information and the public can view the latest clinic information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Children's Privacy

The app is a clinic information application. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage. Google's services may process information according to Google's applicable privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.
